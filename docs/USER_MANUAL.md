# TELEPORTAL Comprehensive User Manual

Welcome to **TELEPORTAL**, the next-generation AI-powered internship management and career acceleration ecosystem.

---

## 1. Student / Candidate User Guide

### 1.1 Getting Started & Registration
1. Navigate to the homepage and click **Join Platform**.
2. Select **Student / Candidate** tab and complete registration with your name, college, email, and branch.
3. Once registered, navigate to your **Student Dashboard** to monitor applications, view interview calls, and inspect recommended internships.

### 1.2 Enhancing Your Profile & Proof of Work
- Visit **My Profile & Links** from the top menu.
- Add your technical skills (e.g., `Python, React, Docker, Machine Learning`).
- Link your GitHub, LeetCode, Codeforces, and personal portfolio URLs. TELEPORTAL automatically incorporates these into employer candidate profiles.

### 1.3 Navigating Internships & Micro-Bounties
- **Internships Board**: Filter by domain (AI, Software, Cloud), mode (Virtual, On-site, Hybrid), and minimum stipend. Each posting displays an instant **AI Match Score** calculated against your registered skills.
- **Proof-of-Work Bounties**: Stand out by completing real corporate micro-tasks. Submit your pull request link to earn direct interview fast-tracks.

### 1.4 AI Career Accelerator Suite
- **ATS Resume Scorer**: Paste your resume draft to receive an instant ATS score, missing keyword detection, and structural suggestions.
- **Tailored Cover Letter Generator**: Generate bespoke cover letters matched to any employer and job description in seconds.
- **Mock Interview Simulator**: Practice Technical and HR questions. Use your microphone or keyboard to answer, receiving instant scoring based on the STAR methodology.
- **Skill Gap Radar**: Select your target job title (e.g., DevOps Engineer) to receive a customized 4-week learning roadmap.

## 2. Employer & Recruiter Guide

### 2.1 Posting Internships & SLAs
1. Click **Post Internship** from the employer control panel.
2. Specify title, category, duration, stipend, required skills, and responsibilities.
3. Review submissions on your **Applicants** dashboard, pre-sorted by our AI candidate compatibility score.

### 2.2 Managing the Candidate Pipeline
- Move candidates across standard stages: `Applied` &rarr; `Under Review` &rarr; `Shortlisted` &rarr; `Interview Scheduled` &rarr; `Accepted`.
- Directly schedule Google Meet / Teams technical interviews with automated calendar reminders sent to the student.

### 2.3 NEP 2020 Academic Credit Evaluation
- For accepted interns completing their tenure, click **Evaluate Intern**.
- Enter attendance percentage, technical competence, professionalism, and project completion marks.
- The system automatically compiles the composite score, assigns the NEP letter grade ($O, A+, A, B$), and issues official academic credits for the student's university transcript.

## 3. Administrator & University Governance Guide

### 3.1 Control Center & Telemetry
- Access `/admin/dashboard` to inspect platform-wide metrics: total registered users, active postings, and aggregate application flow.
- Review Chart.js demographic charts and live security audit logs.

### 3.2 User Moderation & Compliance
- Under **Manage Users**, filter accounts by role.
- Suspend suspicious or non-compliant accounts with one click.

### 3.3 Opportunity Moderation & System Broadcasts
- Review and toggle the active status of any corporate listing under **Moderate Internships**.
- Broadcast platform-wide announcements or role-targeted advisories using the **Broadcast Communications** tool.
